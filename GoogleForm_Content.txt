===============================================
 GOOGLE FORM — HealthSync AI Wellness Survey
===============================================

FORM TITLE: HealthSync AI - Wellness Assessment Survey
FORM DESCRIPTION: We are building an AI-powered wellness assessment platform. This short survey helps us understand your health tracking habits and wellness awareness. Takes less than 2 minutes.

-----------------------------------------------

FIELD 1: Email Address
  Type: Short text (Email validation ON)
  Required: Yes

FIELD 2: Which category best describes you?
  Type: Multiple Choice
  Required: Yes
  Options:
    - Student
    - Working Professional
    - Corporate Employee
    - Other: _____________ (allow custom text input)

QUESTION 1: How often do you get a full health check-up (blood work, lipid panel, etc.)?
  Type: Multiple Choice
  Required: Yes
  Options:
    a) Every year
    b) Once every 2-3 years
    c) Only when I feel something is wrong
    d) I have never had one

QUESTION 2: When you receive a lab report, how easy is it for you to understand the results?
  Type: Multiple Choice
  Required: Yes
  Options:
    a) Very easy — I understand all parameters
    b) Somewhat — I understand the basics but not the details
    c) Difficult — I mostly rely on my doctor to explain
    d) I don't bother reading them at all

QUESTION 3: Do you currently use any app or platform to track your health and wellness?
  Type: Multiple Choice
  Required: Yes
  Options:
    a) Yes, I use a dedicated health app (e.g., Apple Health, Google Fit)
    b) I use a fitness tracker/wearable only
    c) I track manually (diary, spreadsheet)
    d) No, I don't track my health at all

QUESTION 4: What is your biggest challenge when it comes to managing your wellness?
  Type: Multiple Choice
  Required: Yes
  Options:
    a) I don't know what my health data means
    b) I get generic advice that doesn't apply to me
    c) I lack motivation or follow-up after check-ups
    d) I don't have time to focus on health

QUESTION 5: How interested would you be in a platform that combines AI analysis with Ayurveda and holistic wellness approaches?
  Type: Linear Scale (1-5)
  Required: Yes
  Labels:
    1 = Not interested at all
    5 = Extremely interested

QUESTION 6: Which features would you find most useful in a wellness platform? (Select all that apply)
  Type: Checkboxes (multiple selection)
  Required: Yes
  Options:
    - AI-powered lab report interpretation
    - Personalized wellness score (0-100)
    - Risk alerts and early warnings
    - Diet and nutrition recommendations
    - Ayurveda/holistic health insights
    - Appointment booking for wellness assessments
    - Organizational wellness programs for schools/corporates
    - Stress and mental health tracking

QUESTION 7: Any additional comments or suggestions for a wellness assessment platform?
  Type: Paragraph (long text)
  Required: No

-----------------------------------------------
HOW TO CREATE ON GOOGLE FORMS:

1. Go to forms.google.com → click "+" (Blank form)
2. Set title and description as above
3. Add each field/question one by one
4. For "Which category" → add options, click the 3 dots → toggle "Add Other"
5. For Question 5 → choose "Linear scale", set 1 to 5
6. For Question 6 → choose "Checkboxes" (not Multiple Choice)
7. For Question 7 → choose "Paragraph"
8. Go to Settings → toggle "Collect email addresses"
9. Click "Send" → copy the link → share

WHERE RESPONSES ARE SAVED:
- Click "Responses" tab at the top of your Google Form
- All responses are saved there automatically
- Click the green Sheets icon to export to a Google Spreadsheet
- You can screenshot the response summary charts for your project report
